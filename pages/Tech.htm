Tech

The best gears in the world of modern cycling and their details.

Shimano’s “Di2”

Di2 stands for “Digital Integrated Intelligence,” Shimano’s electronic shifting system that uses electrical signals instead of mechanical cables to deliver fast, precise, and reliable gear changes across road, MTB, gravel, and e-bikes.

Cycling “Computers”

A cycling computer, or head unit, uses GPS and wireless sensors to show speed, distance, route, power, and heart rate in real time. Many pair with electronic shifting and sync every ride to apps like Strava.

Smart “Trainers”

A smart trainer holds your bike in place and adds resistance electronically, so apps like Zwift can simulate hills and group rides indoors. Built-in power measurement lets you train by watts in any weather.

Power “Sensors”

Power meters measure force in the cranks, pedals, or hubs, while cadence, speed, and heart-rate sensors add more data. Together they turn feel into numbers: watts, FTP, and cadence.
