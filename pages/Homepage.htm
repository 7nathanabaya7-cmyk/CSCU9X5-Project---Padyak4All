About The Site

The core concept of Padjak4All is to be a cyclopedia for everyone. As the website's name states Padyak-4-All where padyak means "to pedal" in Tagalog and 4All to resemble that the website is for the community.

This is an encyclopedia for cyclists, hobbyists and professionals alike. The website will contain information about cycling's history, the latest technology, the powerhouses dominating currently in the sport, and the anatomy of each race held.
